I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu/), advised by [Prof. Nam Sung Kim](https://fast.ece.illinois.edu/). Before joining UIUC in 2021, I received my dual bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from Zhejiang University and UIUC.

<h2 class="home-section-title">💡 Research Interests</h2>
<!-- ### **Research Interests** -->

My research lies at the intersection of computer architecture and networking, with the goal of improving the performance, efficiency, and scalability of modern datacenter infrastructure. My work focuses on intra-host I/O and networking, SmartNIC and NIC microarchitecture, accelerator offloading, and CPU, cache, and memory resource management.
